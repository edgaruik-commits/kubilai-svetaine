We build wood-fired hot tubs in Lithuania

Thermal Heat is a manufacturer with its own production. Every tub is made to order from thermo wood, acrylic or fiberglass and a stainless steel stove, then delivered to your door.

Who we are

Thermal Heat designs and builds wood-fired hot tubs in Marijampolė, Lithuania. We have 7 years of manufacturing experience and make everything in our own workshop, from the wooden cladding to the finished tub.

You configure your tub online: shape, size, wood, liner colour, stove and accessories. We confirm the final price, delivery cost and date by email, then build it to your specification.

What we make

Shapes

Round, square or rectangular tubs in two sizes.

Materials

Thermo wood cladding with an acrylic or fiberglass liner.

Heating

Stainless steel wood-fired stove, inside or outside the tub. An electric 9 kW heater is also available.

Extras

Hydro and air massage, LED lighting, thermal cover, filtration and Wi-Fi control.
